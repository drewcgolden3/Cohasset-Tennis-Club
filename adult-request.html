Before you book

Lessons for every member.

Lessons are open to all members — kids and adults. Choose private (1-on-1), semi-private (2 players), or small group (3–4 players), and pick your favorite pro or let us match you.

Use the checkboxes to show when you're free and we'll do our best to match those times. If your first choice isn't available, we'll suggest other options. Please give 24 hours' notice to cancel.
