Three ways to play

Structured coaching, real match play.

Whether you're picking a racquet back up or chasing a league title, there's a format built for you.

Clinics

Stroke-focused instruction for every level — dedicated beginner options, our popular intermediate 105 clinic, and custom clinics you can build with a few other members.

Play with a Pro

Get into live doubles alongside a member of our coaching staff. With in-match coaching, we address the challenges that only show up under pressure and help you expand your match play.

Competitive Tennis

Men's singles and doubles in in-house and out-of-house match play, plus women's USTA and MITA teams. Structured, competitive, and genuinely fun.

One-on-one

Private lessons,
on your schedule.

Book private, semi-private, or small-group lessons with the pro of your choice — Christo, Sonny, Chizzy, Whitney, or Dylan — or let us match you to the right coach. Tell us your goals and when you're free, and we'll do our best to confirm within two days.

Prefer a standing time? We'll set up recurring weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly lessons so your court is always waiting.
