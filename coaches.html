The Team

Coached by champions.

Grand Slam pedigree, Hall-of-Fame résumés, and Division 1 and professional coaching experience — all in service of one small, friendly club.

BS & EC
Owners since 2002

Bud Schultz &
Elaine Cosseboom

Bud and Elaine have owned and managed the Cohasset Tennis Club since 2002. Elaine played Division 1 tennis at Boston College and holds USPTA Level 1 certification. Bud was a Division 3 All-American, ranked among the top 40 on the ATP Tour, and competed in four Grand Slam tournaments.

He coached world-class players including Ivan Lendl and Pam Shriver, directed the Boston Lobsters, and served as Tennis Director at Longwood Cricket Club. Together, they've crafted a culture at the Cohasset Tennis Club that has created an oasis for its members.

The pros

Your coaching staff.

CS

Christo Schultz

Tennis Director

Over a decade of experience across the collegiate, professional, and club levels. Christo was Head Coach at Brandeis University for six seasons — coaching All-Americans and achieving a program-best No. 8 national ranking in 2021. A Harvard graduate, he earned First-Team All-Ivy honors and reached No. 14 in national collegiate doubles. He also served as Director of Racquet Sports at Charles River Country Club.

Sonny Bisazza

Director of Jr. Tennis · Assistant Pro

Sonny previously directed Kings Grant Tennis Club and has worked at multiple prestigious clubs. He's USPTA certified with extensive experience in red-ball instruction, junior development, and high-performance coaching. He was a MIAA Division 1 State Champion and a Franklin Pierce University MVP.
