Welcome to the club

An oasis for players
of every age and ability.

Owned and run by Bud Schultz and Elaine Cosseboom since 2002, the Cohasset Tennis Club pairs a warm, family-first atmosphere with genuinely world-class coaching. Between them, our pros have played Grand Slams, coached Hall of Famers, and led Division 1 and professional programs.

The result is a club that feels like home the moment you walk in — high-energy junior clinics, thoughtful adult instruction, competitive league play, and a calendar of round robins and socials that keep the courts full all year.

Our story

Four decades of the
game, on one court.

Elaine captained Boston College's Division 1 team and was named to a USTA National Tennis Family of the Year. Bud reached the top 40 on the ATP Tour, played all four Grand Slams, and coached world top-ten players including Ivan Lendl and Pam Shriver.

In 2002 they brought that lifetime of tennis home to Cohasset — and built the culture members love today.
