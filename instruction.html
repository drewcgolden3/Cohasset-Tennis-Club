One-on-one

Private lessons,
adult and junior.

Work with the pro of your choice, or let us match you to the right coach. Three formats, each bookable from 30 to 120 minutes.

Adults & juniors

Private lesson

You and a pro, working on exactly what your game needs. The fastest way to change a stroke, a serve, or a habit.

  • One player, one coach
  • 30 to 120 minutes
  • Standing weekly or bi-weekly times available

Two players

Semi-private

Share a court and a coach with a partner at a similar level — the same focused instruction, split between two.

  • Two players, one coach
  • 30 to 120 minutes
  • Ideal for doubles partners and siblings
Book semi-private ↗
Four players

Pro + 3

Live doubles alongside a member of the coaching staff, with in-match coaching for the problems that only show up under pressure.

  • Three players plus a pro
  • Real match situations, coached live
  • Great for groups who already play together

Junior program

Big energy.
Small groups.

Our junior clinics meet kids exactly where they are — from a first red-ball class to high-school match prep. A 6:1 cap means no child waits in line for a turn.

Led by Sonny Bisazza, a USPTA-certified junior-development specialist, MIAA Division 1 State Champion and Franklin Pierce MVP.
