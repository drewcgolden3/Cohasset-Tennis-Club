Membership enquiry

Come and see
the club first.

We're a small, local club, so we like to meet people before they join. Tell us a little about yourself and we'll arrange a visit — have a look round, meet the pros, and see whether we're the right fit.

How joining works

Three steps.

Send an enquiry

Fill in the form and tell us what you're looking for — courts, coaching, junior programs, or all three.

Visit the club

We'll be in touch to arrange a time. Come and see the courts, meet our staff, and ask whatever you like.

Join

If it's a fit, we'll set up your membership and your online account so you can start booking.

Not sure which membership suits you? Compare the tiers — but there's no need to decide before you visit.
