The program

Big energy.
Small groups.

Our junior program meets kids exactly where they are — from a first-ever red-ball class to high-school match prep. Coaches keep it fast, fun, and full of reps, while the 6:1 cap means no child is ever waiting in a long line for their turn.

Lessons run $32 each across four seasonal sessions, and Friday point-play clinics reinforce every skill in real match scenarios.
