Big energy.
Small groups.

Our junior program meets kids exactly where they are — from a first red-ball class to high-school match prep — with a six-to-one cap so nobody waits in line for a turn.

The program

A pathway,
not a drop-in.

Players move through red, orange, green and yellow ball as their game grows, so every clinic is pitched at the level they are actually playing.

Led by Sonny Bisazza, a USPTA-certified junior-development specialist, MIAA Division 1 State Champion and Franklin Pierce MVP.

One-on-one

Private lessons
for juniors.

Clinics build the game; a private lesson fixes the one thing holding it back. Book with the pro your child already knows, or let us match them to the right coach.

One player

Private lesson

A single player with a pro, working on the stroke, the serve, or the habit that clinic time cannot reach.

  • One player, one coach
  • 30 to 120 minutes
  • Standing weekly or bi-weekly times available

Two players

Semi-private

Two juniors at a similar level share a court and a coach — often siblings, or two friends who already practise together.

  • Two players, one coach
  • 30 to 120 minutes
  • Ideal for siblings and practice partners

Match prep

High-school prep

Focused work ahead of a school season or a tournament — serve, return, and the points that decide close matches.

  • Built around the season ahead
  • Live point play with a coach
  • Individually or in a small group
