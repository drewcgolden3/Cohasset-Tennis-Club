Junior trial membership

A lower-commitment way for a young player to try the club — full access to junior clinics and programs for a year, so you can see how they take to it before stepping up to a full membership.

Request a trial →

Rates shown are annual. Once your membership is active, courts, clinics and lessons are booked in the CourtReserve member portal — court reservations open 24 hours ahead so our pros can schedule lessons around them.
