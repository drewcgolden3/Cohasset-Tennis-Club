Our Story · Since 2002

An oasis for the game,
on the South Shore.

Two lifetimes in tennis — a Grand Slam career and a Division 1 captaincy — brought home to Cohasset and poured into one welcoming club.

The founders

Bud & Elaine.

Bud Schultz and Elaine Cosseboom have owned and managed the Cohasset Tennis Club since 2002. Between them they bring more than four decades of tennis experience — as players, as coaches, and as stewards of the game.

Elaine ranked among the top junior players in New England and captained Boston College's Division 1 women's team. Her family was named USTA National Tennis Family of the Year, and she's a USPTA Level 1 certified instructor and a Gold Ball recipient in Super Senior Father-Daughter competition.

Together, they've crafted a culture at the Cohasset Tennis Club that has created an oasis for its members.

A life in the game

Bud Schultz's
tennis journey.

From the ATP Tour to the coaching box of world top-ten players, and back to a small club on the coast he calls home.

Player

Top-40 ATP, four Grand Slams

A Division 3 All-American who reached the top 40 in the world and competed at Wimbledon, the French, Australian, and US Opens.

Coach

Lendl, Shriver & Rusedski

Coached world top-ten players, directed the Boston Lobsters (2009–2013), and served as Director of Tennis at Longwood Cricket Club (1990–2002).

Legacy

Hall of Fame & Tenacity

A member of the New England and Intercollegiate Tennis Halls of Fame, and co-founder of Tenacity — an urban tennis and education program serving 5,000+ Boston youth.
